QUESTIONS

Quick answers.

Is it free?

Yes. The core app is free. Plus adds Locked mode, where there is no quit button, and PC pairing.

Can I still get calls and texts?

Yes. You pick which apps get blocked. Everything else works as normal.

What if I really need to get out?

In normal mode you can end a session any time. In Locked mode your partner can let you out, or you get one emergency exit a week.

Can SwipeLater see what I do on my phone?

No. Apple's Screen Time keeps that data on your device. We never get it.
