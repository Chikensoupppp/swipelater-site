Is it free?

Yes, the main app is free. Plus adds Locked mode (no quit button) and PC pairing.

Will I still get calls and texts?

Yes. You choose which apps get locked. Everything else works like normal.

What if I really need to get out?

In normal mode you can end a session whenever you want. In Locked mode a friend can let you out, or you can use your one emergency exit a week.

Can SwipeLater see what I do on my phone?

No. Apple's Screen Time keeps that on your phone, and we never see it.
