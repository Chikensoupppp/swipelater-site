Questions? Answers.

Is it free?

Yes. Locking apps, the timer, your weekly goal, streaks, a Key Holder and the Windows app are all free. Plus adds Locked mode, Lock by itself and your whole history.

Will I still get calls and texts?

Yes. You choose which apps get locked. Everything else works like normal.

What if I really need to get out?

In a normal session you can end it whenever you want. In Locked mode, your Key Holder can let you out, or you can use your one emergency exit a week.

Can SwipeLater see what I do on my phone?

No. Apple’s Screen Time keeps your app list and your usage on your iPhone, and SwipeLater never sees them. The app only sends anonymous usage stats, like which screens people reach.
