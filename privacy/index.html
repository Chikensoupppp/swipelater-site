Privacy

SwipeLater (iPhone app) and SwipeLater for Windows · Updated November 20, 2026

Short version: SwipeLater has no accounts, no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites. Your focus history stays on your devices. To make the app better, the iPhone app sends anonymous usage events (for example, which first-run slides you saw and whether a session finished) to our analytics service, PostHog, under a random ID. We never sell anything. Everything else that leaves your phone is what you choose to share with a partner or your own PC, described below.

What stays on your devices

Your sessions, weekly goal, streak and reminders are saved on your iPhone, and so are the answers you give in the first-run slides (some of those answers are also sent as usage events, see below). On your PC, a small week summary and settings are saved in your Windows user folder. Deleting the app deletes them.

Usage analytics

The iPhone app sends events about how it is used to PostHog (posthog.com, servers in the United States), so we can see which parts work and which confuse people. Each event is tied to a random ID the app makes when it is installed, not to your name, email, phone number or Apple ID. The events are:

  • the app being opened, installed or updated, and which first-run slide you are on;
  • the choices you tap in the first-run slides: your hours guess, what pulls you in, what you want more of, the time you pick and the plan you accept;
  • how many apps you picked to block (the number only, never which apps);
  • sessions started, finished or ended early, and how many minutes they were;
  • whether you bought SwipeLater Plus.

Never sent: your Screen Time numbers (Apple does not let the app read them), the names of your apps, anything you share with a partner, and anything from your PC. The events are not used for ads, not sold and not shared with anyone else.

If you are in the EU, EEA, United Kingdom or Switzerland: the app asks you once, before sending anything, whether you want to share these usage events. Our legal basis is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Saying no changes nothing about how the app works. You can switch it off or on at any time with the “Share usage stats” switch at the bottom of the apps list in the app; turning it off stops new events and resets the random ID. The events are processed by PostHog in the United States, under the safeguards PostHog provides for transfers out of Europe. We keep them for as long as they help us improve the app. You can ask us to see or delete the events tied to your install, or object to their use, by writing to help@swipelater.app. Because events are tied to a random ID and not to you, we may not be able to tell which ones are yours; turning the switch off is the surest way to stop them. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Screen Time

SwipeLater uses Apple's Screen Time tools to block the apps you pick during a focus session. Apple keeps this data on your device. SwipeLater cannot read your usage numbers, and none of it is sent anywhere.

Your partner (Key Holder)

If you pair with a partner, the two phones swap security keys in person. While you are in a session, your phone shares a status with your partner through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit public database): whether you are locked in, on a break or free, when the session ends, and, if you have a PC paired, the name of the app or window you are using on it. Private browser windows are included, so your partner sees what you are on. Your partner can also ask your PC to pause an app, and ask to let you out of a session early.

This data is encrypted on your phone so that only your partner's phone can read it. It is stored in Apple's iCloud under random IDs, not under your name, and it is only used to deliver the status, the request and the answer. Sharing with a partner you paired with is always on while you are paired. Remove the partner to stop it.

Your PC

If you pair a PC, your phone and your PC talk directly over your own Wi-Fi with an encrypted connection. They share the session timer, the task name you pick, your week summary, and what the PC reports back (the app in front and how many times the desk buddy nudged you). Nothing goes through our servers, because we have none.

To find your PC, the PC announces itself on your local network (Bonjour) with its name, its network address and the fingerprint of its security certificate. Your phone looks for that. The first time, the PC shows a four-digit code and asks you to click Allow. If you scan the pairing QR code with the Camera app instead, the code carries the same pairing details. SwipeLater uses your camera only for that scan, and does not save any picture.

If you add the SwipeLater browser extension (Chrome or Edge), it notices long, nonstop scrolling on short-video and feed pages so the desk buddy can remind you to get back to work. It only counts scrolling time on your PC and passes that count to the SwipeLater app on the same PC. It does not send page contents, screenshots or what you type anywhere.

Save for Later: when you click the desk buddy to save a video or post for after your session, only the link you chose is saved, encrypted, in your Windows user folder. It is not sent anywhere.

Purchases

SwipeLater Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we do not get your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your purchase is active. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID and your purchase information from Apple. It does not receive your focus data or your name.

Children

SwipeLater does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to delete everything on your iPhone. Remove a partner or a PC in the app to stop sharing with it. The status record in iCloud is refreshed about every 30 seconds while you are in a session and stops updating when you stop. To ask for a record to be deleted, write to the address below.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. Questions: help@swipelater.app.
