Set up SwipeLater on your PC

4 short steps. No Windows warning. About 5 minutes.

Step 1 of 4 · On your PC

Install SwipeLater

  1. 1Press Get SwipeLater above. It copies the install command for you.
  2. 2On your keyboard, press Win + R. A small box called Run opens.
  3. 3Press Ctrl + V, then Enter. Wait while it downloads. A SwipeLater window opens.
Stuck? The Run box only opens with the Win key (the Windows logo key, bottom left of your keyboard). No Run box? Press Start, type Run, press Enter.

Step 2 of 4 · PC and phone

Pair your phone

  1. 1In the SwipeLater window on your PC, press PAIR MY PHONE. A square code appears.
  2. 2Open the Camera app on your iPhone. Point it at the square code.
  3. 3Tap the SwipeLater banner that pops up on your phone. SwipeLater opens and says it is paired.
Stuck? Your phone and PC must be on the same Wi-Fi. The code runs out after a few minutes. Press NEW CODE to get another.

Step 3 of 4 · In your browser

Connect your browser Optional

This lets your buddy see when scrolling takes over. You can skip it and do it later.

  1. 1In the SwipeLater window, press CONNECT BROWSERS. Chrome opens its extensions page.
  2. 2Turn on Developer mode. It is the switch at the top right.
  3. 3Press Load unpacked.
  4. 4Click the folder box and press Ctrl + V. SwipeLater already copied the folder for you. Then press Select Folder.
  5. 5Find the SwipeLater buddy card. Press Details, then turn on Allow in Incognito.
Stuck? No Developer mode switch? You are on the wrong page. The address must say chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions in Edge).

Step 4 of 4 · On your phone

Start focusing

  1. 1Open SwipeLater on your iPhone. Pick your minutes, then slide to lock in.
  2. 2Look at your PC. The SwipeLater window shows the same timer. If you scroll or open a distracting app, your buddy nudges you.
Stuck? The PC window says No session? Check that the phone still says Paired. If not, go back to step 2.
